## Design

- Model input text form (what `:context` prints and the test rebuilds), exactly: each message renders as `"--- " + role + " ---\n" + escape(content)`, where `role` is `system`, `user` or `assistant` and `escape` is the Scope (6) escape (hidden characters and backslashes); the rendered messages are joined with `"\n"`; the result has no trailing newline (the REPL's `print` adds the one line end). Nothing else is added: no blank line between messages, no header or footer.
- Parser output: a small typed value (`command` + `risk`, or `clarify` / `refuse` + `reason`) or a rejection with a reason for the log; nothing in between.
